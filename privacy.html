Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

🔒 Short version: Invista POS is a desktop application that runs entirely on your computer. We do not collect your business data, customer records, or sales information. Your data never leaves your machine.

1. What We Collect

When you purchase Invista POS or contact us for support, we may collect:

  • Email address — provided voluntarily during purchase, used to send your download link and license key
  • Name — provided voluntarily during purchase
  • WhatsApp messages — when you contact us for support

We do not collect any data from inside the application itself.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

Invista POS has no internet connection requirement and no telemetry. We never collect:

  • Your customer data or contact information
  • Your sales records or transaction history
  • Your inventory or product data
  • Your employee information
  • Your location or device information
  • Usage statistics or analytics

3. How We Use Your Information

The limited information we collect (email, name) is used solely to:

  • Deliver your purchase (download link and license key)
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important updates about the software you purchased

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Payment Processing

Payments are processed through Binance Pay. We do not store your payment details. Please refer to Binance's privacy policy for information about how they handle payment data.

5. Your Data on Your Device

All business data created within Invista POS — including customers, products, sales, and employees — is stored locally in a database file on your computer (invista.db). This file never leaves your machine unless you choose to back it up manually.

6. Data Retention

We retain your email and name for as long as you are an active customer. You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp.

7. Security

Since we collect minimal data, our exposure is minimal. Your purchase information is kept privately and is never publicly accessible.

8. Children's Privacy

Invista POS is a business software product not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will notify you of significant changes via WhatsApp or email if you are an existing customer.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us on WhatsApp: +961 70 000 655
